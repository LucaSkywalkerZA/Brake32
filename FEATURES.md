# Brake32

Wireless brake temperature monitoring for track days and performance driving.
Thermocouple probes mount at the brakes, a small sender box transmits the
readings, and you watch them live — on your phone's lap-timing app, on the
optional in-vehicle display, or both.

## Brake temperature sensors

- 0–1,100 °C range
- 10 updates per second, 0.1 °C resolution
- 2 channels standard (pad + caliper, or left/right) — expandable to 8
- Genuine K-type thermocouples, fibreglass high-temperature leads
- Probe leads 2 m or 5 m (custom on request)
- Plug-in probe connectors — swap in seconds at pad changes
- Only passive probe wire at the wheel — no electronics near the heat
- Runs on 12 V vehicle power, no batteries
- Transmits wirelessly over standard protocols — works with regular lap
  timing / monitoring apps (RaceChrono, BLE sensor monitors) with or
  without the display
- Sensor faults detected within ~1.5 s; hot readings never falsely dropped
- Uniquely paired units — multiple systems at one event won't interfere

## Optional in-vehicle display

- Live brake temps on a compact colour dash display
- Colour-coded zones: blue = cold, green = good, amber = working, red = back off
- Session min/max always on screen; one-button reset
- Clear OFFLINE / ERROR indicators — you always know the data is real
- Plugs in with USB
